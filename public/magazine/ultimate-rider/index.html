The Weekend DRC Ultimate Rider

Two days. Every surface.

Not just a race.
It’s a test of everything.

DRC Ultimate Rider is a two-day motorsport event built to test riders across different forms of off-road riding — not a single type of terrain. Saturday filters the field. Sunday decides it. The same dirt. One prize pool across the weekend.

Saturday

Qualification · Elimination · Challenges

Saturday is the proving ground: qualification, elimination and multiple challenges across every surface. The day is built to strip the field down. The riders who last the day have not won. They have only earned Sunday.

Sunday

The Final

Sunday is the Final. The field is smaller. The surfaces still change. This is where the weekend is decided — after every rider still standing has already been tested.

The Platform Dirt Ride Camp

That is the belief Dirt Ride Camp was built on. DRC exists because Indian motorcycle culture has always been larger than the space it was given. What was missing was a home: a world around the rider — not a single weekend, and not a single start line.

We are building a motorcycle and adventure world where riding, training, adventure, motorsport, racing, experiences, community, content and brands belong together.
