03 / After Sunday The motorcycle stays

The flags
come down.
The motorcycle
stays.

Give us the bikes. We’ll keep them in the work.

Most race partnerships end when the podium is packed. This one is built to outlive the weekend. The official motorcycles don’t go back in a crate on Monday. They stay with DRC — ridden on trails, used in training, lived with on adventure, filmed because they are still being used.

That is the advertising that doesn’t look like advertising. A rider on your machine, on dirt, after the race is over. Again. And again.

Trails

DRC keeps riding. The partner motorcycle keeps going out — on routes, on dirt, in the hands of riders who weren’t on the start list.

Training

The same machine in the next pair of boots. New riders learn on it. The brand stays in their hands, not only on a barrier.

Adventure

Camp. Dawn. Days that are not a start gate. The motorcycle is still there when the fire is lit and the helmets are on the ground.

Content

Because the bikes stay in the work, they stay in the pictures. Race week is the flag-off. The trails keep the story in motion.
